# Shopper Nigeria Launch — "Your Shop, Online Today"

**Window:** 90 days, starting week of 12 Oct 2026 (Weeks 1–13)
**Market:** Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan first — the densest Instagram/WhatsApp seller populations)
**Budget:** Phase 1 (Weeks 1–6) organic, ₦0 media · Phase 2 (Weeks 7–13) paid tests, up to ₦200,000/month on Meta
**Site:** https://www.shopperafrica.com · **Instagram:** @shopperafricacms

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Name:** *Your Shop, Online Today* (hashtag **#MyShopIsOnline**)

**One-line summary:** Show Nigerian Instagram and WhatsApp sellers that they can turn their DMs into a real online store — with checkout, delivery fees and order tracking — in 10 minutes, for free.

**Primary objective (conversion):**
**500 new stores created and 150 "activated" stores** (at least 1 product + 1 real order) by the end of Week 13.

**Secondary objectives:**
| Objective | Target by Week 13 |
|---|---|
| Free → paid (Starter/Business) conversions | **30 paying stores** (~₦300k+ MRR) |
| Instagram followers @shopperafricacms | +2,000 |
| Organic search sessions to marketing site | 1,500/month by Week 13 |
| Comparison-page sign-ups (vs Bumpa / Shopify / Wix / WhatsApp-only) | 50 |

> Why these numbers: with ₦0 for six weeks and ≤₦200k/month after, ~5–6 sign-ups a day is achievable from content + communities + a modest Meta test. Activation (30%) and paid conversion (~6% of sign-ups, ~20% of activated) are the numbers that actually matter — sign-ups that never list a product are noise.

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary — "The DM seller"
> A Nigerian small-business owner (22–40, often a woman, often a side business) who sells fashion, beauty, hair, food, gadgets or home items through **Instagram and WhatsApp**, struggling with **answering the same price questions all day, retyping price lists, chasing "I've paid" screenshots and losing orders in chats**, and looking for **a proper store link that looks professional and takes orders for them**. They discover tools through **Instagram/TikTok creators, WhatsApp groups, friends who sell, and Google searches like "how to create online store in Nigeria"**, and care most about **cost, ease (phone-only), and getting paid safely**.

- **Pain points:** "DM for price" fatigue · no time to build a website · fear of monthly dollar subscriptions · developers are expensive/unreliable · customers don't trust IG-only sellers · bank-transfer confirmation chaos
- **Motivations:** look legit · sell while asleep · stop losing orders · run promos easily · get paid by card
- **Buying stage:** mostly **unaware → problem-aware** (they don't think "e-commerce platform", they think "I'm tired of DMs")
- **Where they are:** Instagram (feed, Reels, Stories), TikTok, WhatsApp status & groups, Twitter/X (Nigerian "small business Twitter"), Nairaland, Facebook seller groups, Jiji/Konga sellers, Google

### Secondary — "The switcher"
> An established seller already on **Bumpa, Shopify, Wix, WordPress/WooCommerce or Selar** who is frustrated by **cost (often dollar-priced), complexity, plugins, or a developer they depend on**, and is looking for **a cheaper, simpler, WhatsApp-native option**. They search Google for "**Bumpa alternative**", "**Shopify alternative Nigeria**", "**cheap website for my business Nigeria**".

- **Buying stage:** consideration/decision — they convert fastest and are most likely to pay (Starter)

### Tertiary — "Influencers & agencies"
Social media managers, small digital agencies and business coaches who set up stores for clients — one partner can bring 10–50 stores (see Partner programme, §4).

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:**
> **Turn your DMs into a real online store in 10 minutes — free to start, built for WhatsApp, and you get paid properly.**

| # | Supporting message | Addresses | Proof point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Stop answering "how much?" all day.** One link with every product, price and option — customers order themselves. | DM fatigue | Live demo store (The Wave) — show a full order in a 30-second screen recording |
| 2 | **Orders land on your WhatsApp, already written out.** Name, items, delivery fee, total, address — no retyping. | Order chaos | The structured WhatsApp order message (screenshot) |
| 3 | **Free to start, priced in naira.** Free plan forever; card payments + your own domain from ₦10,000/month. No dollar bills, no plugins, no developer. | Cost fear | Pricing page; "no card required" |
| 4 | **Look legit, get paid safely.** Paystack card payments, delivery fees, order emails and a receipt page your customer can trust. | Trust / payment | Checkout + receipt screenshots, Paystack logo |

**Tone by channel**
- **Instagram/TikTok:** relatable, a little funny, Nigerian English/Pidgin sprinkles ("Abeg stop retyping price list 😩"), fast cuts, faces
- **WhatsApp status/groups:** short, personal, link-first
- **Google/SEO pages:** clear, factual, comparison-driven
- **Paid ads:** one pain → one promise → one CTA ("Create your free store")

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Sign-ups don't activate** (create store, never add products) | Onboarding emails + WhatsApp nudge from founder to every new store in first 48h; "Free setup Friday"; track activation weekly, not just sign-ups |
| **Organic reach too low in Phase 1** | Lean on communities, partners and WhatsApp (not algorithm-dependent); post Reels at 7–9pm WAT; collab posts with sellers |
| **Meta ads attract low-quality sign-ups** | Optimise for *activated* stores, not clicks; target with business-owner interests + lookalikes of activated stores; retarget engaged viewers only |
| **Trust objection** ("new platform, will my money be safe?") | Paystack branding, real customer stories, founder face on camera, clear "nothing is ever deleted" promise |
| **Competitor response / price comparison** | Compete on WhatsApp-native + naira pricing + simplicity; keep comparison pages factual and dated |
